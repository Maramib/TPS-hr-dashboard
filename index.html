The Challenges

HR Problems
We're Addressing

Two challenges are confirmed at TPS. The remaining three are common HR pain points observed across the industry — included as a broader view for consideration.

Confirmed at TPS Suggested — industry-common risk
📄
Poor Job Descriptions
At TPS, BA roles (Level 1/2/3) and seniority levels share near-identical JDs with no clear differentiation in responsibilities, expectations, or KPIs. This leads to mismatched applicants from the start.
⚠ Confirmed at TPS — affects every hiring cycle
⏱
Manual & Slow Screening
HR receives a high volume of resumes and reviews them manually. With no automation, the right candidate can be missed simply due to the scale of applications — not lack of qualification.
⚠ Confirmed at TPS — high volume, manual effort
🚪
Weak Onboarding Experience
Without a structured onboarding program, new hires often lack clarity on their role, tools, and expectations in the first weeks. This increases early attrition risk and slows time-to-productivity.
⚡ Suggested — common across growing tech teams
📉
Low Employee Engagement Visibility
HR teams without regular pulse surveys or feedback mechanisms have limited visibility into team morale — making it hard to act before issues escalate into turnover.
⚡ Suggested — often invisible until it's too late
🎯
No KPI Framework for Roles
BA and analyst roles at many organizations lack defined, measurable KPIs tied to the job description. Performance reviews become subjective and inconsistent across managers and cycles.
📊 Suggested — directly tied to the JD problem above

Next Steps

Recommended Path Forward

A phased approach to embedding AI into HR operations — starting where the pain is highest.

01
Deploy JD Review Tool
Roll out the AI JD reviewer to all hiring managers. Establish a quality benchmark and collect feedback for iteration.
02
Pilot an HR AI Platform
Run a 90-day pilot with either watsonx Orchestrate or Talenteria to cover screening and onboarding automation.
03
Expand to Engagement & KPIs
Integrate Macorva or Leena AI for pulse surveys and performance tracking. Build a full-cycle AI HR stack.
